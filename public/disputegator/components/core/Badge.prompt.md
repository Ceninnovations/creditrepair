Small status pill for priority (High/Medium/Low), dispute strength (Strong/Moderate/Weak), and rating labels (Fair).

```jsx
<Badge tone="high">High</Badge>
<Badge tone="strong">Strong</Badge>
<Badge tone="fair">Fair</Badge>
<Badge tone="positive" icon="trending">Impact: Positive</Badge>
```

- Tone maps to the semantic color pairs: high→red, medium→amber, low/positive→green.
- Always wrap one short word/phrase; never multiline.
