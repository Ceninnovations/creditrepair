Colored round avatar for a credit bureau — its two-letter abbreviation on the brand color.

```jsx
<BureauMark bureau="experian" size={44} />
<BureauMark bureau="equifax" />
<BureauMark bureau="transunion" size={34} />
```

- Pass a bureau key (`experian`/`equifax`/`transunion`) or a full `BureauInfo`.
- Brand colors: Experian `#2f6df0`, Equifax `#c0202e`, TransUnion `#1c9aa8`. Import `BUREAUS` for the registry.
