# DisputeGator — product context

**DisputeGator is a complete credit & financial wellness product, not just a credit-dispute tool.** The journey spans four pillars:
- **Credit Plan** — find & dispute errors on the credit report
- **Payoff Plan** — pay down balances/debt
- **Budget Builder** — manage income vs. obligations
- **Grow & Rebuild** — build positive credit and long-term financial health

When writing copy, frame the experience around this whole journey — improving credit *and* financial future — not only disputing.

## Tone / copy
- Do **not** use the word "attack" (or "attacking"). The brand is supportive and empowering, not combative.
- Brand identity: green "gator" mascot, supportive coach voice. User in mock data: Chad Nicely (Premium Member).
